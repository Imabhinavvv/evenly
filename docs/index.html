Privacy Policy

Evenly (com.algorithmaliens.evenly) • Last updated: November 20, 2026

Evenly is built by Algorithm Aliens as an offline-first expense splitting app for roommates, trips, and friends. We believe your private financial records shouldn't sit on a company server or be tracked.

The short version: We don't collect, store, or sell any of your data. The app works entirely offline on your device, with no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

Where Your Data Goes

Everything you enter into the app—names, expenses, split amounts, and groups—is stored locally in your device's internal storage (localStorage). None of it is uploaded or transmitted to any server.

Permissions We Ask For

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Only used when you choose to scan a physical receipt or scan a peer-to-peer transfer QR code. Text extraction runs 100% on-device using Google ML Kit. Camera frames are processed in temporary memory and immediately discarded. No images or videos are ever saved or uploaded.
  • Files & Storage: Only used when you explicitly export a PDF report, CSV spreadsheet, or .evenly backup file to your device.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Standard Android requirement to run the local web engine. Evenly does not make network calls to any analytics, tracking, or telemetry services.

Peer-to-Peer Sharing

When you share an expense log using an in-app QR code or single .evenly file, the transfer happens directly between your device and the recipient (via camera scan, Bluetooth, or your chosen messaging app). Only that specific log is shared; your other groups and history remain private.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual expenses, members, groups, or history entries at any time inside the app. To delete all data permanently, clear Evenly's storage in your Android settings or uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Evenly does not include advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics frameworks (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no cookies).

Children's Privacy

Evenly does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
